RB-Validity - Satisfied
The broadcaster sends <type0, m> to all. Since reliable message-passing channels ensure that all processes receive it and deliver it, RB-Validity holds.

RB-Agreement - Satisfied
If one process delivers m, then the message was sent to all, so all processes eventually receive and deliver it. This is ensured by the protocol pseudo-code where if a message m is not in the delivered set, the process adds m to the delivered set and triggers delivery of m.

RB-Integrity - Satisfied
The delivered set prevents duplicate delivery. Since messages are not created by the channel and only exist when they are received, the delivered set ensures that a message will be delivered at most once. Therefore, RB-Integrity holds.
