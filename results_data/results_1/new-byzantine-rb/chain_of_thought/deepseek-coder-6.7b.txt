RB-Validity - [Satisfied]
The broadcaster is sending <type0, m> to neighbours and only once per message. Correct receivers are sending <type1, m> to their neighbours after receiving at least one valid <type0, m> from a correct process, and this process continues until it receives f + 1 distinct <type1, m> messages from correct processes. Therefore, all correct processes eventually deliver the message m.

RB-Agreement - [Satisfied]
If one correct process delivers m, then at least one of those received f + 1 valid <type1, m> messages. These messages are spread by a correct process through its neighbours and this spreading continues until all other correct processes also receive the same messages. Therefore, all correct processes eventually deliver m.

RB-Integrity - [Satisfied]
A correct process delivers m at most once because of the 'delivered' set. Since f + 1 valid <type1, m> messages are required and only f Byzantine processes can send these messages (as per invariant I2), a Byzantine process alone cannot force delivery. In addition, an incorrect process will not be able to deliver m as it does not possess the necessary support of correct processes.
