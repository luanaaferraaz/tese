RB-Validity - satisfied

RB-Agreement - satisfied, the process must receive at least f + 1 valid messages from correct processes to deliver a message. There is no reason for this property not being satisfied.

RB-Integrity - satisfied, a correct process delivers only once because it keeps track of delivered messages in a set. If a Byzantine process tries to force the delivery by sending multiple <type1, m> messages, it will be detected and prevented due to the use of the 'delivered' set.
