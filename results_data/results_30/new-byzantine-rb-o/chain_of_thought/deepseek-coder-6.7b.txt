RB-Validity - Satisfied: If a correct broadcaster sends <type0, m> to its neighbours, all correct processes eventually receive at least one of them and send <type1, m> to their neighbours. This is ensured by the protocol in step B where each time a <type0, m> message is received from a process, it checks if there are enough distinct messages (i.e., f + 1) that have been sent before and not yet delivered. If this condition holds true, it sends out a <type1, m> message to its neighbours.

RB-Agreement - Satisfied: If one correct process delivers m, then all correct processes eventually receive at least f + 1 <type1, m> messages from different correct processes. This is ensured by the protocol in step B where each time a <type0, m> message is received from a process, it checks if there are enough distinct messages (i.e., f + 1) that have been sent before and not yet delivered. If this condition holds true, it sends out a <type1, m> message to its neighbours.

RB-Integrity - Satisfied: A correct process delivers m at most once because of the 'delivered' set. Since f + 1 support is required (which is ensured by RB-Agreement), a Byzantine process alone cannot force delivery, as it would require more than f valid senders from different processes and hence can be detected.
