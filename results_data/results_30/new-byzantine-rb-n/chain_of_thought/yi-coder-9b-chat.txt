1. RB-Validity - The algorithm satisfies the RB-Validity property because, if a correct process broadcasts a message, all other processes also deliver this message within a finite time period given that f = 1 and n ≥ 4 as per the assumptions.
2. RB-Agreement - The RB-Agreement property is satisfied by this algorithm since once one correct process delivers a message m, it receives at least f + 1 support messages from other processes. This support will be propagated among all other correct processes, leading to agreement on delivering m.
3. RB-Integrity - The integrity property holds true in this algorithm as a process does not deliver any message more than once and only if the message was broadcasted. A Byzantine process cannot force delivery of a message because only f + 1 distinct processes are required for this.
