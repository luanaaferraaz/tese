RB-Validity - [satisfied]
Correct processes receive f + 1 <type1, m> messages and deliver. They sent these messages to their neighbours, so correct broadcaster eventually delivers valid m.

RB-Agreement - [satisfied]
One correct process sends at most once per message because of the delivered set. If a process delivers m, it received f + 1 <type1, m> from other processes. These messages can only be sent by correct processes, so all correct processes eventually deliver m.

RB-Termination - [satisfied]
All correct processes always deliver because they receive at least f + 1 <type1, m> messages. The delivery threshold is not achievable by only f Byzantine process, so termination holds.
