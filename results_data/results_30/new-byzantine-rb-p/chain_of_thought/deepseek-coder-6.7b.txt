RB-Validity - Satisfied
The broadcaster sends <type0, m> to its neighbours and correct receivers send <type1, m> to their neighbours. As long as a correct process receives at least f + 1 <type1, m> messages from different processes (which is the case due to the protocol), it eventually delivers m.

RB-Agreement - Satisfied
If one correct process delivers m, then it received f + 1 <type1, m> messages. At least one of those came from a correct process. The forwarding rule spreads this support to the other correct processes, allowing them to reach the same delivery threshold and eventually deliver m.

RB-Termination - Satisfied
All correct processes always deliver because they send <type1, m> messages only when they have received at least f + 1 <type0, m> or <type1, m> messages from different processes. As long as a correct process receives enough support to reach the delivery threshold (f + 1), it eventually delivers m.
