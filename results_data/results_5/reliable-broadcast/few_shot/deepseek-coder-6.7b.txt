Algorithm: Reliable Broadcast (RB)

Properties:
1. Validity - [satisfied]
   The algorithm correctly ensures that if a correct process broadcasts m, then it eventually delivers m. 
2. Agreement - [non satisfied]: if sender crashes mid-broadcast, some correct processes may never receive m
3. No duplication - [satisfied]
   Every correct process delivers m at most once ensuring no message is relayed more than once.
4. No creation - [satisfied]
   If a correct process delivers m, then m was broadcast by some process. This property ensures that no new messages are created. 

Please note that the Validity and Agreement properties may not hold true if there are f<n faulty processes in the system where f is less than n (maximum faulty processes). These processes can crash midway through the broadcast, causing correct processes to miss some or all deliveries of messages. The algorithm's efficiency relies on a reliable network and that no more than max f processes can fail during a single broadcast operation.
