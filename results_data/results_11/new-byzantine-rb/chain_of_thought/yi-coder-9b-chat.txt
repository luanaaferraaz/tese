RB-Validity - [satisfied]
This algorithm ensures that a correct process broadcasts m, then all other processes eventually deliver m. The <type1, m> messages serve as evidence for delivery.

RB-Agreement - [satisfied]
If one correct process delivers m, it receives f + 1 <type1, m> messages from other correct processes. This means at least one of those supports was generated by a correct process and forwarded to the other correct processes in a chain that ensures agreement among all correct processes.

RB-Integrity - [satisfied]
A process only delivers once because of the delivered set. A Byzantine process cannot force delivery since f + 1 support is needed, which requires at least one correct process's support and can be propagated to other correct processes.
