1. RB-Validity: This property is satisfied because the algorithm ensures that all correct processes eventually deliver m, even if the broadcaster is correct. The message m will be relayed and delivered by the correct processes when at least f + 1 distinct <type1, m> messages are received.
2. RB-Agreement: This property can also be verified as satisfied because once one correct process delivers m, it receives f + 1 support messages from distinct senders. The rule for spreading support guarantees that all other correct processes will eventually reach the delivery threshold and deliver m as well.
3. RB-Integrity: Finally, this property is also satisfied by this algorithm due to the delivered set implementation which prevents a process from delivering the same message more than once.
