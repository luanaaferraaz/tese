RB-Validity - Satisfied
The broadcaster sends the initial message <type0, m> to its neighbours. The receivers of this message send support messages <type1, m> to their neighbours. After receiving at least f + 1 distinct valid <type1, m> messages from correct processes, a process delivers m and triggers the output event DELIVER(m). 

RB-Agreement - Satisfied
If one correct process delivers m, it received f + 1 distinct valid <type1, m> messages. This means at least one of those came from a correct process. Therefore, this support can be spread to the other correct processes, allowing them to reach the same delivery threshold and deliver m. 

RB-Termination - Satisfied
All correct processes eventually receive at least f + 1 <type1, m> messages. This ensures that all correct processes always deliver a message once they have received sufficient support (f + 1 valid <type1, m> messages). So, the termination property is satisfied for this algorithm.

The Delivered set ensures each message gets delivered only once which seems to fulfill the requirement of no duplicate delivery (I4).
