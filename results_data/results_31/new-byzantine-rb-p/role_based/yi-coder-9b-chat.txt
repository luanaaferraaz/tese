1) RB-Validity - Satisfied. The algorithm guarantees that any correct process that broadcasts a message will eventually deliver it, and all other processes will eventually also deliver this message.

2) RB-Agreement - Satisfied. The agreement property is met if at least one correct process delivers the message. All other correct processes will then also deliver the message due to their support propagation mechanism.

3) RB-Termination - Satisfied. The algorithm does not have any infinite loops or failures, it ensures that all correct processes eventually deliver a message they receive.
