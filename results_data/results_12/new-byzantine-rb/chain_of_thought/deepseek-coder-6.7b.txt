Here are the final results for each property of this algorithm:

1. RB-Validity - Satisfied
   The broadcaster sends a <type0, m> message to its neighbours if it is not already sent. This message will be received and forwarded by all correct receivers. As such, all correct processes eventually deliver the message.

2. RB-Agreement - Satisfied
   If one correct process delivers a message, then it receives at least f + 1 <type1, m> messages from other correct processes. The delivery threshold of 2 messages ensures that this agreement is maintained and that all correct processes eventually deliver the same message.

3. RB-Integrity - Satisfied
   A process delivers a message at most once because it maintains a delivered set. Since f = 1, it means at least one non-Byzantine process must support the message for delivery. If all Byzantine processes cooperate by sending duplicate <type1, m> messages, this would violate the integrity property. Hence, only distinct valid senders can deliver a message.
