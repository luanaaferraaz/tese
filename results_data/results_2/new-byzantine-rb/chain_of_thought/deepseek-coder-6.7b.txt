RB-Validity - [satisfied]
- The broadcaster sends <type0, m> to its neighbours. Correct receivers then send <type1, m> to their neighbours. Since correct support spreads, correct processes eventually receive at least f + 1 <type1, m> messages and deliver m.

RB-Agreement - [satisfied]
- If one correct process delivers m, it received f + 1 <type1, m> messages from the same or different correct processes. The forwarding rule spreads this support to other correct processes, allowing them to reach the same delivery threshold and deliver m.

RB-Integrity - [satisfied]
- A process delivers only once because of the delivered set. Since f + 1 support is required and at least one process can be Byzantine, a Byzantine process alone cannot force delivery. Delivered messages are checked to avoid delivering the same message multiple times.
