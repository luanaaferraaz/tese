1. Validity - [satisfied]
    If a correct process broadcasts a message m, then every correct process eventually delivers m. This is because the algorithm relies on perfect point-to-point links which ensure reliable and eventual reliability of messages in the presence of failures.
2. No duplication - [satisfied]
    No message is delivered more than once. This is guaranteed by the uniqueness constraint imposed on messages, as well as the "no duplication" property of the underlying perfect links.
3. No creation - [satisfied]
   If a process delivers a message m with sender s, then m was previously broadcast by process s. This property follows directly from the fact that in this best-effort broadcast algorithm, all correct processes receive messages solely through their underlying perfect point-to-point links.
