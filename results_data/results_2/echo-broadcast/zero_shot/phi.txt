 The Bracha's Reliable Broadcast algorithm is designed to handle Byzantine faults in asynchronous, reliable channels. It achieves this through the use of quorum thresholds and counters for messages received from each process. If sent_ready and delivered are true for all processes at the same time, it is a successful broadcast. Otherwise, it will fail due to the presence of at least one faulty process. This algorithm has been proven to be reliable in asynchronous environments by computer scientists.
