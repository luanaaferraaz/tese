Validity - [satisfied]
Reason: The algorithm ensures that if a correct process broadcasts a message, all other correct processes will eventually acknowledge it.

Agreement - [non satisfied]
Reason: Although the algorithm guarantees that each process acknowledges every unique message broadcast by at least one correct process, there is no guarantee that the messages are identical across different processes.

No duplication - [satisfied]
Reason: The algorithm ensures that a single unique message will not be acknowledged more than once.

No creation - [non satisfied]
Reason: There's no rule in the algorithm to ensure that a message created by one process is indeed broadcasted by this same process, which may introduce potential issues of non-creation.
