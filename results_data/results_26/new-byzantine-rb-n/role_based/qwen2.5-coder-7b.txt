RB-Validity - [satisfied]: Correct receivers send <type1, m> to neighbours, ensuring all correct processes receive at least f + 1 distinct <type1, m> messages.
RB-Agreement - [satisfied]: At least one process delivers m, and its correct support spreads, allowing others to reach the same delivery threshold.
RB-Integrity - [satisfied]: A process delivers m only once because f + 1 support is required, preventing Byzantine processes from forcing delivery.
