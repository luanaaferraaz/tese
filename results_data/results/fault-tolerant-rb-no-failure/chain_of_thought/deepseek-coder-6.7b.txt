RB-Validity - Satisfied
The broadcaster sends <type0, m> to all processes. This is ensured by the first step of protocol pseudocode (A) where a message is sent only if it has not been sent before. 

RB-Agreement - Satisfied
If one process delivers m, then the message was sent to all, so all processes eventually receive and deliver it. This is ensured by the second step of protocol pseudocode (B) where a message is delivered only if it has not been delivered before. 

RB-Integrity - Satisfied
The delivered set prevents duplicate delivery. Since messages are not created by the channel, delivered messages come from a broadcast. This ensures that each message is delivered at most once and no duplicates are introduced.
