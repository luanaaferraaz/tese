Correctness Properties:

1) Termination
The protocol ends at round f+1, and each process delivers exactly one message.

2) Validity
If s is correct (no omission failures), all processes eventually receive m from it.

3) Agreement
Each process delivers a value v if and only if every process delivers v. This follows from the fact that once f+1 correct supporters have been accumulated, the protocol decides to deliver the message received_msg.

4) Integrity
Each process delivers at most one message (either m or SF), and that is either received directly from s in round 1, or relayed by a correct process.
