1. Validity - [satisfied]
The correct process guarantees that it will eventually deliver the message it broadcasts.

2. No duplication - [satisfied]
Each correct process checks if a delivered message is already in its delivered set before delivering it or forwarding it to other processes. This ensures each process delivers every correct process' broadcast at most once.

3. No creation - [satisfied]
In a fault-tolerant distributed system, the message was either broadcast by some process or delivered from another process that is assumed to be correct (non-faulty). Therefore, this property is satisfied in our algorithm.

4. Agreement - [satisfied]
After a correct process delivers a message m, it immediately sends m to all other processes under the assumption of reliable communication channels and correct processes only. Every other correct process eventually receives m and delivers it due to the asynchronous nature of the model. Therefore, all correct processes will eventually agree on the messages they deliver.
