Algorithm: Reliable Broadcast (RB)

Properties: Validity, No duplication, No creation, Agreement

Output:
Validity - [satisfied]
No duplication - [satisfied]: The delivered set prevents duplicate deliveries.
No creation - [satisfied]: Since the algorithm uses reliable channels and honest processes, no message can be created that is not broadcasted by some process.
Agreement - [non satisfied]: In the current implementation of Reliable Broadcast, if a correct process delivers m, it immediately sends m to all other processes. However, in case of a crash, there might exist scenarios where some correct processes may never receive or deliver m due to the asynchronous nature of the system.
